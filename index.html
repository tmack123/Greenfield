GreenField

Soil, weather and market information for farmers, in one place.

GreenField is a web app that helps farmers in Ogun State estimate their soil's nutrient status and crop suitability, follow weather-based farming advice, and sell produce directly to buyers. It runs in any phone browser and can be installed without an app store.

02 · Our solution

Objectives of the study

Main objective: to develop GreenField, a digital agricultural extension platform for improving farmers’ access to soil, weather, and market information.

The specific objectives are to:

  1. Design and build a soil assessment module capable of estimating soil type and crop suitability from soil characteristics that farmers can observe, such as colour, texture, and drainage.
  2. Develop a digital interface that delivers localised weather forecasts alongside actionable crop advisory and farm management practices.
  3. Integrate a marketplace module that bridges the gap between farmers, buyers, and other agricultural actors for direct trade and communication.
  4. Evaluate the usability and effectiveness of the system within a rural extension framework.
03 · How it works

Three tools, one app

  1. 🧪

    Assess your soil

    Choose your soil's colour and texture, and optionally add a photo and field notes. GreenField returns estimated pH, N, P, K and organic carbon levels, suitable crops, and fertilizer and management advice.

  2. 🌤

    Plan with the weather

    Pick a location in Ogun State or nearby. The app shows a 7-day forecast from Open-Meteo, with rule-based advice on planting, spraying, irrigation and harvesting.

  3. 🛒

    Buy and sell produce

    Farmers sign in and post produce with a photo, price and location. Buyers search and filter the listings, and contact the seller directly on WhatsApp.

  4. 📲

    Install and stay updated

    Add GreenField to your phone's home screen from the browser. Saved soil analyses follow your account, and announcements appear in Alerts.

04 · Benefits

Who it helps

👩🏾‍🌾Farmers

  • A quick first look at soil fertility and crop suitability
  • Weather advice in plain language, tied to farm tasks
  • A place to advertise produce and reach buyers directly

🧺Buyers

  • Browse produce by category, price and location
  • Contact sellers on WhatsApp in one tap
  • Save listings to compare later

📣Extension workers

  • Send announcements to every user from the admin panel
  • A shared tool to support farm visits and advice
  • Moderate marketplace listings
Scope and limitations: soil results are estimates based on what can be seen and felt in the field. They do not replace laboratory soil testing. Weather advice depends on the forecast data, which can change.
